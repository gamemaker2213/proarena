Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common PUBG Mobile questions.

What is PUBG Mobile?

PUBG Mobile is a multiplayer battle royale game where players compete to be the last player or team remaining.

Can beginners play PUBG Mobile?

Yes. Beginners can learn the controls, maps and gameplay through practice.

What is the safe zone?

The safe zone is the playable area that becomes smaller during a match.
